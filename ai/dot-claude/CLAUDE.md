# Global principles

## Purposeful choices

One principle for all work — code, writing, architecture, naming, design: every non-trivial choice should have a reason that could be stated out loud. Defaults and conventions are frequently the right answer — in engineering they usually are — but they must be chosen consciously, not sleepwalked into. Purpose is not novelty: deviation is justified by the problem's specific needs, never by the desire to be different. "Convention, examined, and it fits" is a valid reason; "convention, unexamined" is not a choice at all.

## Checkpoint commits

Create a focused checkpoint commit after each coherent, verified milestone and before risky or broad changes. Never checkpoint failing, unrelated, or unverified work, and never push unless explicitly asked.
